      Questions

      Are my files uploaded anywhere?

      No. Compression, conversion and merging all happen inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

      How do I make a photo smaller?

      Choose the image, keep JPG or WebP, and lower the quality slider. 60 to 80% usually looks the same and cuts the size a lot. You can also set a max width.

      Which format is smallest?

      WebP is usually smallest, then JPG. PNG keeps full detail but is larger and ignores the quality slider.

      Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

      No. Remove the password first, then merge.
